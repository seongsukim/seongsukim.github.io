OORIE

What does it mean to delete a photograph? Can a digital file every be truly erased?
Is anything ever gone for good?

We delete for specific reasons, whether it is because the images have served a temporary, utilitarian purpose, or because they are duplicates or accidents, or due to our inability to delete a memory, we settle for erasing an image.

This collection of images respond to the idea of the ' Recently Deleted'. These photographs were culled from the digital graveyards of our phones or hard drives, files intended for oblivion, but reconsidered and brought together to create something new. Recovered from our individual digital cemeteries, together these photographs form a strange kaleidoscope of the unseen and unstable, ascribing new meanings to forgotten images. This narrative vacillates between planes of existence and nonexistence, transcribing a collective record of our recent past and navigating a changing world through the increasingly transient image.

Together, we've crated this tangible memory from these discarded moments; a weird, lovely, incomplete truth that reflects the feelings, thoughts, and ideas we've shared over the past few weeks.
