## Introduction

I've come across many patterns in my life. Firstly, our society is one form of a pattern, and in our society people and objects make it harmonious. In particular, distinct shapes of various sculptures and commodities have triggered my curiosity to begin this work. Air conditioning filters, Hydrant microphone, sewers, lockers, notice boards etc. There are too many objects to mention which create patterns. I wanted it to be shown to emphasize simply on the shape of the objects. Some people may see it as beautiful, some people may not…

## Air Conditioner

One day I went into the cafe and looked up at the ceiling. There was an air conditioner which had never been used before. It was a rectangular air conditioner. There was a lot of holes in the middle of the air conditioner, and it was a filter. Suddenly I thought of it. What makes the shape of the filter so shaped and why it is so beautiful, it really appeals to me. And the more interesting thing is, the company made different shapes for each company that made the air conditioner.

From that day on, it was a habit to check the company's identity if it found an air conditioner somewhere.
